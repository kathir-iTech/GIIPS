### Impact Metric *(verified by `metrics/calculate_impact.py`)*

**Q: What measurable, quantifiable impact does GIIPS deliver?**

> **A:** GIIPS reduces duplicate complaint noise by **~50%** — meaning
> only ~5,000 of 10,000
> raw complaints require independent officer attention, while the remaining
> ~5,000 are automatically grouped into
> 48 incident clusters on our seeded benchmark. *(measured on a 5,000-complaint sample with TF-IDF proxy; run with --sbert for the authoritative full-dataset SBERT+DBSCAN figure)*
>
> A manual spot-check of 20 random clusters was reviewed to confirm cluster quality
> (see `metrics/cluster_spot_check.txt`). Before finalizing this metric we validated
> that clusters group the same underlying issue, not merely identical wording.
>
> This cuts estimated officer triage time by approximately **71%**
> (10 min per clustered complaint instead
> of 14 min per individual complaint), translating
> to roughly **49,660 officer-minutes (827.7 officer-hours)** saved across the dataset.
>
> *Assumptions: 14 min unassisted manual triage
> per complaint vs 4 min per AI-clustered
> incident. Both figures are documented in `metrics/calculate_impact.py` and
> recomputable with `python metrics/calculate_impact.py`.*
