# DEMO_SCRIPT.md

## Live Demo Script (Duration: 5-7 Minutes)

### Phase 1: The Problem (1 Minute)
*   **Intro**: "Municipalities receive thousands of complaints daily. Staff are overwhelmed manually reading and categorizing these, leading to huge duplication and delayed responses."
*   **Action**: Briefly show the raw data structure if needed or just explain the volume problem.

### Phase 2: The Solution - AI Pipeline (2 Minutes)
*   **Action**: Go to the **Citizen Portal** and submit a complaint about a "burst water pipe".
*   **Action**: Immediately navigate to the **Incident Feed**.
*   **Highlight**: "Notice the system didn't just add a complaint; it analyzed it, classified it, and grouped it with existing duplicate reports into a single, high-priority **Incident**."
*   **Highlight**: "This is how we achieve 85% workload reduction."

### Phase 3: Prioritization & Intelligence (2 Minutes)
*   **Action**: Open the **Cluster Explorer** for the new incident.
*   **Highlight**: "Here, we see the individual complaints linked to this incident. Our AI calculated the priority based on cluster size and location criticality."
*   **Action**: Switch to **Executive Dashboard**.
*   **Highlight**: "This gives the city commissioner a 30,000-foot view of city health and department workload."

### Phase 4: Future Insights (1 Minute)
*   **Action**: Go to **Spatial Intelligence**.
*   **Highlight**: "We map this data geographically to identify hotspots and forecast future demand, allowing for proactive, rather than reactive, resource allocation."

### Phase 5: Conclusion (1 Minute)
*   **Conclusion**: "GIIPS transforms grievance redressal from a reactive, manual burden into a proactive, AI-driven intelligence system."
